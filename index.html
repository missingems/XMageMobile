Install on This iPhone
  1. Open this page in Safari on a registered device.
  2. Tap Install, then confirm. The app appears on your Home Screen.
  3. If it won’t open, turn on Settings › Privacy & Security › Developer Mode.
  4. In the app, set the bridge host to the Mac running the bridge.
