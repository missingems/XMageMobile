XMage Duo Privacy Policy

Last updated 9 November 2026

What the app collects

Nothing. XMage Duo has no accounts of its own, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The developer receives no data from the app.

Servers you choose

To play, the app connects to an XMage game server you pick (or a bridge you run on your own Mac or home server). It sends that server the player name you type and your game actions. That server is run by its own operator, whose policies apply.

Card images

Card images and card data are loaded from Scryfall. Those requests include card names, not anything about you.

Stored on your device

Your server address, player name and deck choices are saved only on your iPhone, and are removed when you delete the app.
